Classic Matcha Latte

A smooth and creamy matcha latte made with Oatside milk, perfect for everyday matcha cravings.

Ingredients

  • 200 ml Oatside Milk
  • 1 tsp Matcha Powder of LittleRetailPH
  • 1–1½ tbsp Condensed Milk
  • Ice cubes

Procedure

  1. Pour Oatside milk into a glass.
  2. In a separate cup, dissolve matcha powder with a small amount of water.
  3. Whisk until smooth.
  4. Add condensed milk and mix well.
  5. Add ice into the milk.
  6. Pour the matcha mixture on top.
  7. Serve and enjoy.

Brown Sugar Matcha

A soft and creamy matcha drink with sweet brown sugar syrup and beautiful layers.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp Brown Sugar
  • 200 ml Oatside Milk
  • 1 tsp Matcha Powder of LittleRetailPH
  • 1 tbsp Condensed Milk
  • Ice cubes

Procedure

  1. Mix sugar with a small amount of water.
  2. Microwave for 30 seconds until it becomes syrup.
  3. Pour syrup into a glass.
  4. Add Oatside milk.
  5. Prepare matcha separately and mix with condensed milk.
  6. Add ice.
  7. Pour matcha mixture on top.

Dirty Matcha

A bold matcha drink topped with foamy coffee for a unique café-style twist.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp Instant Coffee
  • 200 ml Oatside Milk
  • 1 tsp Matcha Powder of LittleRetailPH
  • 2 tbsp Condensed Milk
  • Ice cubes

Procedure

  1. Pour Oatside milk into a glass.
  2. Prepare matcha in a separate cup and mix with condensed milk.
  3. Add ice into the milk.
  4. Pour the matcha mixture on top.
  5. Dissolve coffee with a small amount of water.
  6. Froth until slightly foamy.
  7. Pour the coffee foam on top.
  8. Serve immediately.

Matcha Mug Cookie

A quick and soft matcha snack that is easy to make in one mug.

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp All-purpose Flour
  • 2 tbsp Sugar
  • ½ tsp Matcha Powder of LittleRetailPH
  • ¼ tsp Baking Soda
  • 1 tbsp Milk
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • Pinch of Salt
  • Chocolate Chips

Procedure

  1. In a mug, mix flour, sugar, matcha powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. Add milk and oil, then mix until smooth.
  3. Add chocolate chips on top.
  4. Microwave for 1 minute and 30 seconds.
  5. Let it cool slightly.
  6. Serve and enjoy.

Matcha Pastillas

A sweet bite-sized matcha treat that is simple, soft, and easy to prepare.

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp Bear Brand Milk Powder
  • 2 tbsp Matcha Powder of LittleRetailPH
  • 3 tbsp Condensed Milk
  • White Sugar (for coating)

Procedure

  1. Mix milk powder and matcha powder in a bowl.
  2. Add condensed milk and mix until it forms a soft dough.
  3. Grease your hands lightly with oil.
  4. Scoop and roll the mixture into small balls or logs.
  5. Coat each piece with sugar.
  6. Refrigerate for at least 3 hours.
  7. Serve chilled.

Soft Matcha Truffles

A soft and rich matcha dessert with a smooth texture and elegant flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 bars Goya White Chocolate
  • 2 tbsp Milk
  • 1 tsp Matcha Powder of LittleRetailPH
  • 1 tbsp Milk Powder

Procedure

  1. Melt the white chocolate using microwave or double boiler.
  2. Add matcha powder and mix well.
  3. Add milk and mix until smooth.
  4. Pour the mixture into a mold or container.
  5. In a separate bowl, mix milk powder and matcha for topping.
  6. Sprinkle the topping on the chocolate mixture.
  7. Refrigerate for 10–12 hours until firm.
  8. Cut or shape and serve chilled.

Matcha Cheese Toast

A simple but satisfying snack with cheesy and matcha flavors in one bite.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices Bread
  • Mozzarella Cheese
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 1 cup All-purpose Flour
  • 1 small Egg
  • Splash of Milk
  • ½ tsp Matcha Powder of LittleRetailPH

Procedure

  1. In a bowl, mix butter, flour, egg, milk, and matcha powder until smooth.
  2. Slice the bread into 2–4 pieces.
  3. Place mozzarella cheese between two slices of bread.
  4. Dip or coat the sandwich with the matcha mixture.
  5. Microwave for about 1 minute or until cooked.
  6. Serve warm and enjoy.

Matcha Ice Candy

A cold and creamy frozen matcha dessert that is perfect for hot days.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp Matcha Powder of LittleRetailPH
  • 2 tbsp Hot Water
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 2–3 tbsp Condensed Milk
  • Ice candy plastic
  • Rubber bands

Procedure

  1. In a cup, dissolve matcha powder with hot water.
  2. Whisk until smooth and no lumps.
  3. Add milk and condensed milk, then mix well.
  4. Taste and adjust sweetness if needed.
  5. Pour the mixture into ice candy plastic using a funnel.
  6. Tie each plastic securely with rubber bands.
  7. Freeze for at least 6–8 hours or until solid.
  8. Serve cold and enjoy.
