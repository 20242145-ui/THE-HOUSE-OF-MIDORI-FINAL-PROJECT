Stores

This section includes popular stores with branches around the Philippines. They offer accessible and consistent matcha drinks you can enjoy anytime.

Tavern Cafe

Photo by Tavern Cafe via Facebook

One of the best cafés with a breathtaking view, especially during sunset. During foggy days, it gets really cold, so bringing a jacket is a must. They offer a variety of drinks, including matcha, along with good food. Perfect for relaxing and enjoying the scenery..

Location: CH29+3X8, Pine Tree Alley, Baguio, Benguet

Tavern Café tavern_cafeph

Foam Coffee

Photo by Foam Coffee via Facebook

This café offers a variety of matcha flavors, and every peso you spend here is worth it. You can enjoy high-quality matcha drinks while relaxing in a calm and cozy atmosphere. It is also a student-friendly café because they provide free internet and charging ports, making it a great place to study or review your lessons.

Location: 15 Bukaneg St, Baguio, Benguet / Shuntug Rd, Baguio, Benguet

Foam Coffee foamcoffeeph Branch 1 - Bukaneg Branch 2 - Shuntug

Uji Matcha Café

Photo by Uji Matcha Cafe via Facebook

This place has a calm and relaxing vibe, perfect for students who want to study or review. They offer different matcha flavors made with high-quality matcha powder, so you can really taste the difference.

Location: Lower Bonifacio St, Baguio, Benguet

Uji Matcha

Nono's Guidos and Ristorante

Photo by Nono's Guidos and Ristorante via Facebook

A café with a forest-like vibe where you can relax and enjoy the view. It’s very Instagrammable and peaceful. They serve a variety of drinks, including matcha, along with different food options.

Location: Bektey, Longlong, Puguis, La Trinidad, Benguet

Nonno Guido's Ristorante

MOCH Cafe and Bistro

Photo by Moch Cafe and Bistro via Facebook

One of the best places to enjoy matcha while relaxing. It has a beautiful view and serves high-quality food and drinks. A great spot if you want a peaceful café experience.

Location: Gulf View Horizon, Suello Village, Tuba, Benguet

Moch Cafe

Cornelia Street Bistro House

Photo by Cornelia Street Bistro House via Facebook

A Taylor Swift–inspired café filled with themed decorations and collections. The place is fun and unique, and they also serve matcha drinks. Definitely worth the visit for both the vibe and the experience.

Location: 3 Navy Rd, Baguio, Benguet

Cornelia Street Bistro House

Itaewon Creative Lounge

Photo by Itaewon Creative Lounge via Facebook

An Instagrammable café with a modern and stylish setup. They offer a variety of drinks, including matcha, and it’s also student-friendly place.

Location: Mabini St, Baguio, Benguet

Cafe Moonlight

Photo by Cafe Moonlight via Facebook

A calm and cozy place that’s perfect for spending time alone. They offer matcha drinks and food, and the peaceful vibe makes it a great place to unwind.

Location: Session Rd, Baguio, Benguet

Sunndown Coffee La Union

Photo by Sundown Coffee La Union via Facebook

Perfect for tourists visiting Elyu. Their matcha is great for beginners—not too strong but creamy and smooth. The prices are affordable, and the place is chill, making it perfect for catching up with friends or loved ones.

Location: Urbiztondo Rd, San Juan, La Union

Stream of Consciousness

Photo by Ryza Francisco

A good option for non-matcha lovers since the flavor is more on the creamy side. It’s a bit pricey, but worth it because the place is very Instagrammable. Great for hanging out and chatting with friends.

Location: San Fernando City, La Union

EsKape Bongabon

Photo by EsKape Bongabon via Facebook

This café made me fall in love with matcha. It’s located in our hometown and offers a variety of drinks, but their match a really stands out. The place is peaceful and not crowded, perfect for alone time.

Location: Bongabon, Nueva Ecija

Kape Nuevo

Photo by Kape Nuevo via Facebook

One of the best cafés in our hometown. Their matcha is really good, and the place has a perfect sunset view. It’s best to visit in the afternoon and just relax while enjoying your drink.

Location: Bongabon, Nueva Ecija

Rohi Paperflower & Cafe

Photo by Rohi Paperflower & Cafe via Facebook

They offer different matcha flavors, and everything is worth the price. The place feels relaxing, and every corner is Instagrammable. Not just the matcha—even their food is really good.

Location: Bongabon, Nueva Ecija

Gondolla Coffee

Photo by Gondolla Coffee via Facebook

A quiet café with a homey vibe. They serve drinks and snacks, but their matcha stands out. Perfect for people who prefer a peaceful and less crowded place.

Location: Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija

CHIN CAFE

Photo by CHIN CAFE via Facebook

A spacious and Instagrammable café. They offer a wide variety of food and drinks, and their matcha is something even non-matcha lovers can enjoy.

Location: Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija

The Coffee Box

Photo by The Coffee Box via Facebook

A calm and cozy place, perfect for bonding with friends and family. They offer different food and drinks. If you prefer a sweeter matcha flavor, this is a good place to try.

Location: Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija

James' Blend Cafe

Photo by James' Blend Cafe via Facebook

Perfect for students. You can study while enjoying your matcha. They offer affordable drinks with different flavors, and it’s a great choice if you prefer a quiet and not crowded café.

Location: Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija

Taste from the Greens Philippines

Photo by Taste from the Greens Philippines via Facebook

They offer a variety of drinks, and their matcha is rich and umami. You can customize your sugar and ice level, so you get the taste you prefer. Definitely worth trying.

Location: Find the nearest branch to you.

ChaTime Philippines

Photo by ChaTime Philippines via Facebook

They offer many drink flavors, but their cream cheese matcha is my favorite. It really stands out from the menu, and you can easily find a branch near you.

Location: Find the nearest branch to you.

Starbucks Philippines

Photo by Starbucks Philippines via Facebook

A well-known store that offers customizable drinks. I usually order matcha here because you can adjust it based on your preference by adding different ingredients. It’s also very accessible since they have branches almost everywhere.

Location: Find the nearest branch to you.
